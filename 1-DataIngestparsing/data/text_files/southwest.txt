Southwest Airlines Co. is a major American airline headquartered in Dallas, Texas. It is the world's largest low-cost carrier, known for its unique business model and customer-friendly policies. Southwest operates an extensive domestic route network, serving over 100 destinations across the United States and some international locations. The airline is recognized for its distinctive boarding process, which allows passengers to choose their seats on a first-come, first-served basis. Southwest's commitment to low fares, friendly service, and operational efficiency has made it a popular choice among travelers and has contributed to its long-standing success in the competitive airline industry.
